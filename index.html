Privacy Policy

Status Saver • com.xapp.waveleap.statusaver • Updated: 21 Nov 2026

Status Saver is an independent utility from X App Team that saves status files locally from folders you select. X App Team does not receive your saved status files or WhatsApp account data. Status Saver does not require an account or login. The app has no analytics or advertising SDK and does not request Android internet permission. Optional online tools open external applications as described below.

1. Data Handling & What We Do Not Collect

  • Direct Chat (Phone Number): If you choose to use the optional Direct Chat feature, the recipient phone number and optional message you enter are formatted on your device and passed to the selected WhatsApp app through its click-to-chat link when you tap Send. Status Saver remembers up to five recently opened phone numbers on this device for reuse. It does not keep a history of the messages you enter. X App Team does not receive the phone numbers or messages.
  • No Developer Collection of Personal or Contact Data: X App Team does not collect or store your name, email address, contacts, or saved media on our servers.
  • Status files saved from the folders you select are not uploaded to X App Team. Saving uses Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and MediaStore on your device.
  • No tracking, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics or ads SDKs (AdMob/IronSource/Facebook removed).

2. Permissions We Request & Why

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below): To save statuses in public Downloads and show files saved by the app. These permissions are not requested on Android 10 and later.
  • Storage Access Framework (Android/media): To read, save copies of, and delete selected status files after you choose the folder in Android's system picker. Deletion requires confirmation in the app. The granted URI is stored locally in app preferences. You can revoke the folder grant in Android settings or by clearing app data.
  • Download feedback: Status saving shows an on-device confirmation. The app does not request notification, camera, microphone, contacts, location, broad media-library, or all-files permissions.

You may skip setup and use tools without granting folder access. Without access to a status folder, Recent cannot show its files. Android 9 and earlier require storage permission to save files to public Downloads.

3. Local Status Processing

Status files are copied from the folder you grant to Download/Status Saver/Images or Download/Status Saver/Videos. On Android 10 and later, saved files are managed through MediaStore; older versions use the public Downloads folder. Optional auto-save copies viewed files when the app loads or refreshes your selected folders and keeps a local record of filenames. The video splitter reads only the video you select, processes it on the device, and writes the clips to Download/Status Saver/Videos. Share and repost hand the selected files to an external app.

4. Third-Party Services

The optional Web Chat shortcut opens https://web.whatsapp.com/ in an external browser, not inside this app. You may need to enable the browser’s desktop-site setting and link your WhatsApp account there. WhatsApp and your browser handle any account information, cookies, connection data, messages, and downloads under their own policies. Status Saver does not create or store a WhatsApp login session. See WhatsApp’s privacy policy.

Direct Chat sends the phone number and optional message you enter to an external WhatsApp link only when you tap its action button. Share/Repost sends the file you select to the external app you choose. Those services may transmit this data over the internet under their own policies; X App Team does not receive it.

Rate and More Apps open Google Play or an external browser when you tap them. We do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics. The app itself has no internet permission.

5. Children's Privacy

Status Saver is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. X App Team does not knowingly collect personal data from children. External services have their own age requirements.

6. Security

Persisted folder grants are managed by Android. Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences from the device, although Android backup settings may back up and restore app preferences, including recent Direct Chat numbers.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

X App Team does not operate a server that stores your app data. To remove local files, delete them in the app or Android's file manager. To revoke folder access or clear recent Direct Chat numbers from Status Saver, clear its app data in Android Settings. Web Chat sessions are stored in your external browser; use its controls to clear them and WhatsApp’s Linked devices screen to unlink a session. For data handled by WhatsApp, use WhatsApp’s account and privacy controls. For questions about this app, contact us below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect Android API changes. The updated date and policy will be posted here.
