Privacy Policy

Waveleap Status Saver • com.xapp.waveleap.statusaver • Updated: 23 Sep 2026

Waveleap Status Saver (X App Team) respects your privacy. This is an offline utility that helps you save WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business statuses. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account, no login, no analytics, no ads SDK.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone, contacts) is collected.
  • No status content is uploaded to any server. All saving/sharing happens locally on device via Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and MediaStore.
  • No tracking, no advertising IDs, and no third-party analytics or ads SDKs (AdMob/IronSource/Facebook removed).

2. Permissions We Request & Why

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below): To save statuses in public Downloads and show files saved by the app. These permissions are not requested on Android 10 and later.
  • Storage Access Framework (Android/media): To read the status folders for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business after you choose the folder in Android's system picker. The granted URI is stored locally in app preferences. You can revoke the folder grant in Android settings or by clearing app data.
  • Download feedback: The app shows an on-device confirmation after a WA Web download. On Android 12 and below it may also show a local download notification. No push notification permission is requested.

All permissions are optional. Denying only limits related feature (e.g., without SAF you will see empty Recent).

3. Local Processing Only

Status files are copied from the folder you grant to Download/Status Saver/Images or Download/Status Saver/Videos. On Android 10 and later, saved files are managed through MediaStore; older versions use the public Downloads folder. No network call is made for status-file access. Share/Repost uses Android content URIs and WhatsApp intents.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party analytics or ads. Links to Play Store (Rate, More Apps → X App Team) open in external browser only when you tap them.

5. Children's Privacy

App contains no user-generated content collection and is suitable for general audience. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Security

Persisted folder grants are managed by Android. Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences from the device, although Android backup settings may restore app preferences if you reinstall.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

As no personal data is processed, there is nothing to access or delete on servers. To revoke storage access: Android Settings → Apps → Waveleap Status Saver → Permissions, or clear app data. For questions: contact us below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect Android API changes. The new date will be posted here. Continued use after update implies acceptance.
